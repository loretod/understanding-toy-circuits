Explore the joystick’s X and Y readings

Drag the joystick and watch the numbers change. Left and right changes X. Up and down changes Y. When you let go, both settle back near the middle.

Keyboard: arrow keys move, Shift + arrows for small steps, Space presses down, Home returns to center.
